You can teleport anywhere on Earth. So you jump from Los Angeles to London... and slam sideways into a wall. Earth spins. LA moves at about fourteen hundred kilometres an hour. London moves slower... in a different direction. You arrive still carrying LA's speed. Over two thousand kilometres an hour, sideways. And appearing where air already is shoves it aside with a thunderclap. Try the top of Everest instead? The air is so thin you'd pass out in about two minutes. The only safe jumps are short ones. So you grab your coffee in LA... and teleport to London.
