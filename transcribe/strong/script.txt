You just became a hundred times stronger. You could lift a car over your head. But your spine didn't get stronger. The car's weight would crush it. Your tendons didn't either. Your muscles would rip them right off your bones. Say your body could take it. Your grip squeezes with about four tonnes. One handshake crushes a hand. Opening a door rips it off its hinges. Try to sprint, and your feet just slip. Shoes can't grip any harder. Jump, and you'd fly about forty metres up... then land at nearly a hundred kilometres an hour. So you take a deep breath... and very gently lift the car.
