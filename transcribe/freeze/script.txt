You snap your fingers. And time stops. Ten seconds. The whole world freezes, except you. Pigeons hang in mid-air. A coffee splash turns to glass. A taxi stops mid-turn. Sounds amazing, right? But there's a problem nobody talks about. To see anything, light has to reach your eyes. Normally, light travels three million kilometres in ten seconds. That's almost eight trips to the Moon. But now, light is frozen too. Not a single bit of it reaches you. So the moment time stops, you go completely blind. And it gets worse. You try to take a step. But every air molecule around you is frozen in place. It's like being sealed inside solid glass. You can't walk. You can't even breathe in. No light. No sound. Just ten silent seconds. Then, time starts again. The splash lands. The pigeons fly off. So, would you still hit pause?
