Get X-ray vision, and you'd be giving everyone you look at a dose of radiation. There's almost no X-ray light down here, so to see, your eyes would have to fire X-rays themselves. Stare at someone for half an hour, and you could burn their skin. And you'd never see bones: hospital X-rays work because the detector sits behind the body. Your lens can't even focus X-rays, so the whole world would be one glowing blur. Would you still take it? Yes or no?
