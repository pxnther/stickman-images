Fall into a volcano, and you wouldn't sink. Lava is three times denser than your body, and a hundred times thicker than honey. So you'd hit it... and just lie on top. But you'd be in trouble long before you landed. The gas above the crater can knock you out in a few breaths. The lava glows at over a thousand degrees. Its heat hits you like two hundred suns. Your clothes catch fire before you even touch it. So if you ever stand at the edge of one... don't lean over.
