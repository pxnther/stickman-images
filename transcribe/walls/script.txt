Walk through walls, and you couldn't even walk. Every atom in your body is 99.99% empty space. So is the wall. The only thing stopping you is electrons pushing back on electrons. Turn that off... and it's not just walls. The floor stops holding you too. You'd sink straight into the ground. Your feet can't push off anything, so you can't take a single step. Light would pass right through your eyes... so you'd be blind. Air would slip through your lungs, so you couldn't breathe. And you'd keep falling, all the way to the center of the Earth. So... would you still want to walk through walls?
