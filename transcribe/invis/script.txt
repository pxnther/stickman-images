You've turned completely invisible. First problem: you're blind. To see, your eyes have to catch light. Invisible eyes let it pass straight through. Second problem: you're not really invisible. Your body bends light like glass, so people see a shimmering outline of you. To vanish completely, you'd have to bend light exactly like air. And that sandwich you just ate? Everyone can watch it float... and slowly get digested inside you. Rain makes it worse. Every drop splashes off your invisible body. So you stand still in the kitchen... and take another bite.
