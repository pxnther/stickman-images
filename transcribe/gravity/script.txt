Gravity just switched off. For five seconds. Second one. Your stomach drops, like the top of a roller coaster. But you don't fly off. Not yet. Earth is still spinning at 1,670 kilometres per hour. Nothing pulls you down anymore, so you keep going straight, while the ground curves away under your feet. You drift up. About forty centimetres in five seconds. Second two. Cars can't brake. No weight means no grip. They just keep rolling. Second three. You try a tiny hop. Bad idea. Two seconds later, you're at the second-floor windows. Second four. The air above you starts drifting off into space. Then, second five. But here's the twist. The danger was never gravity turning off. It's gravity coming back. Everything that drifted up comes down. At once. Including you. Five metres up. You hit the ground at thirty-six kilometres per hour. So if gravity switched off right now... would you hold on, or jump?
