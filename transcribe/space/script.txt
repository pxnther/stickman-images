You've been thrown out of a spaceship with no suit. You don't explode. Your skin holds you together. But don't hold your breath. The air in your lungs would tear them apart. Breathe out... and you have about fifteen seconds before you pass out. The water on your tongue starts to boil. And you won't freeze instantly. Space is a vacuum, so heat leaves you slowly. In direct sunlight, you'd get the worst sunburn of your life. Get pulled back inside within a minute or so, and you'd probably survive. So you reach for the airlock door...
