You can move a thousand times faster than everyone else. To you, the world almost stops. But unlike frozen time, light still reaches you. You can see. Then you take a step. Walking speed, times a thousand, is five thousand kilometres an hour. Four times the speed of sound. The air hits you like a wall. Your skin heats up hotter than an oven. Every step makes a sonic boom that shatters windows. You could even sprint across a lake without sinking. But you're starving. Just speeding up burns about sixteen thousand calories. That's eight days of food, every time you start running. And it gets worse. One day for everyone else lasts almost three years for you. Your friend blinks... and you've grown old. So, would you still want super speed?
