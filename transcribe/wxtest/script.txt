You've shrunk to the size of an ant. One drop of coffee is nearly a hundred times heavier than you. So you jump off the table. To you, that's a fall from a hundred-storey building. You just walk away. Then it starts to rain. Every drop knocks you flat... and water is sticky now. It won't let go. You look up, and everything is blurry. Your pupils are thinner than a hair. Light can't focus. You shout for help. Nobody hears you. Your voice is too high for human ears. And you're getting cold. Fast. Nothing warm-blooded is this small, and now you know why. So you climb back to your coffee... and look up.
